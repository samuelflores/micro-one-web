## Vinnova funding decision
**Document:** Beslutsmeddelande.pdf, decision dated 11 November 2026, reference 2026-03720.

- **Page 1:** planning-grant award, SLU coordination and the project period, 7 November 2026 – 28 September 2027.
- **Page 2:** SLU and Karolinska Institutet as grant recipients; total maximum grant of SEK 499,980.
- **Pages 3–5:** conditions and reporting arrangements. An award is not evidence of disbursement or completion of work.

The funded activity is planning for an international application. The decision does not award funding for the larger Horizon Europe research programme.

## Vinnova application
**Document:** proof.pdf, 49 pages, titled “Microbiome for terrestrial livestock sustainability and health within a One Health approach”. Page references use the PDF’s page order, which matches its printed page numbers.

- **Pages 6–7:** One Health research concept; animal and environmental microbiomes, soil, water, feed and air; wastewater-based epidemiology; antimicrobial resistance; explainable AI, FAIR data and sensors.
- **Page 7:** the planning objective is a competitive Horizon Europe proposal, including engagement with collaborators along zoonotic entry corridors to Europe.
- **Pages 8–19:** the five planning work packages and their schedules, activities and intended outputs.
- **Pages 23–24:** wider scientific objectives, including monitoring, risk modelling, interventions and a data platform.
- **Pages 25–26:** SLU and KI expertise and consortium development. Only SLU and KI are named on this website while the wider consortium is being developed.

The application describes ambitious future research alongside the planning activities. This site presents surveillance systems, sampling, modelling and interventions as proposed research, rather than work already delivered through the planning grant.

## Horizon Europe proposal draft
**Document:** MICRO-ONE-PartB-Draft.2.docx, supplied proposal draft.

The “Specific objectives”, “Conceptual framework” and “Methodological approach” sections support the description of animal and environmental sampling, metagenomics, wastewater-based epidemiology, resistance and zoonotic risk modelling, FAIR data and sensing. The draft is a developing research plan, not an award decision, validated result or confirmation of future participation.

## Project lead’s clarification
The project lead clarified that the site should focus on monitoring livestock disease threats entering Europe, the spread of antibiotic resistance and zoonoses, using metagenomics of soil, water and animals. This sets the editorial emphasis within the broader research concept. No specific border location, sampling network or deployed surveillance service is claimed.

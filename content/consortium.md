## SLU · Coordinator
The **Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU)** coordinates the Vinnova-supported planning project. The application describes expertise in livestock systems and wastewater microbiome surveillance, alongside research infrastructure relevant to livestock studies.

## Karolinska Institutet · Planning partner
**Karolinska Institutet (KI)** is a grant recipient in the planning decision. The research concept draws on expertise in metagenomics, exposome research and health data integration.


## International consortium under development
The planning project is developing the international consortium for a future Horizon Europe proposal, including engagement along zoonotic entry corridors to Europe. The work involves refining scientific roles, identifying gaps in expertise and agreeing how the research could be implemented.

Relevant areas include livestock disease surveillance, metagenomics, environmental sampling, antimicrobial resistance, zoonotic epidemiology, FAIR data, explainable AI and sensors. The wider consortium is not listed here while participation and roles are being developed.


## Joint proposal preparation
The planning work includes consortium workshops, scientific strategy, budgeting, stakeholder engagement and external proposal review. Named institutional participation in this planning project does not imply a Horizon Europe award.

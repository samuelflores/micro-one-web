The funded planning project runs from **7 November 2026 to 28 September 2027**, according to the Vinnova decision. The work packages below follow the application’s activity plan. Dates describe the planned schedule; they are not reports of completed work.

## WP1 · Project Coordination and Grant Management
**7 November 2026 – 28 September 2027**

Coordinate the planning project, organise meetings, monitor progress and risks, maintain documentation and prepare Vinnova reporting. Integrate the scientific, consortium and budget contributions into one proposal-development process.

**Intended outputs:** project management arrangements, meeting records, progress and risk documentation, and coordinated proposal preparation.


## WP2 · Consortium Building and Kick-off Workshop
**7 November – 19 December 2026**

Engage complementary participants, identify gaps in expertise and organise the first consortium workshop. Develop the scientific direction, preliminary work packages, roles and governance for the future proposal.

**Intended outputs:** consortium composition, workshop documentation, a preliminary project concept and an agreed proposal-development roadmap.


## WP3 · Scientific Strategy and Horizon Proposal Development
**30 April – 29 August 2027**

Define research questions, sampling and analytical methods, scientific objectives and the implementation approach. Refine work packages and draft the Excellence, Impact and Implementation sections.

**Intended outputs:** a developed scientific concept, methods and work-package structure, plus an integrated proposal draft.


## WP4 · Budget, Implementation and Proposal Finalisation
**30 April – 28 September 2027**

Integrate the scientific, technical, administrative and financial components. Review the proposal with participants, consolidate the budget and governance, and arrange external expert review and revisions.

**Intended outputs:** a complete proposal package, implementation plan, consolidated budget and documented external review.


## WP5 · Stakeholder Engagement and Final Consortium Meeting
**1 July – 28 September 2027**

Complete quality assurance, confirm participation and institutional approvals, prepare the remaining submission requirements and develop the roadmap for collaboration beyond the planning phase.

**Intended outputs:** a quality-assured proposal, participation confirmations and a collaboration and communication roadmap.


## Future research
Operational sampling, surveillance deployment, model validation and evaluation of disease or resistance outcomes belong to the proposed research programme. They are not reported as funded planning outputs or completed achievements.

## What MICRO-ONE is developing
MICRO-ONE is developing a research programme to monitor livestock disease threats entering Europe, the spread of antibiotic resistance and zoonoses through metagenomic analysis of **soil, water and animals**. Metagenomics analyses genetic material from microbial communities, allowing the proposed research to examine pathogens and resistance alongside the wider microbiome.

The application identifies engagement with collaborators along **zoonotic entry corridors to Europe** as a planning priority. The research would connect animal and environmental observations, including wastewater-based epidemiology, to investigate emerging threats within a One Health framework linking animal, human and environmental health.


## How the proposed monitoring would work
The research design brings together sampling, microbiome profiling and data integration. Soil, water and animal samples are central to the surveillance focus. The application also considers feed, air and human-associated microbiomes, together with wastewater-based epidemiology.

FAIR data infrastructure would support harmonisation, sharing and analysis. Explainable AI would be developed to investigate disease risk, resistance emergence and microbiome–environment relationships. Sensors would provide complementary environmental and physiological measurements. These methods are part of the proposed programme, rather than technologies already delivered by the planning project.

The wider proposal also considers microbiome-based interventions, animal welfare and sustainable livestock production. This site leads with surveillance as the project’s central focus.


## What is funded now
Vinnova, Sweden’s Innovation Agency, awarded a **planning grant** on **11 November 2026**, reference **2026-03720**. The decision specifies a planning period of **7 November 2026 to 28 September 2027**. SLU is the coordinator and Karolinska Institutet is a grant recipient and planning partner.

This support is for developing the international consortium and preparing a Horizon Europe proposal: scientific strategy, work packages, budgets, stakeholder engagement and external review. The grant award does not establish that funds have been disbursed or that particular activities have been completed.

The proposed surveillance research requires future funding. No Horizon Europe award or operational monitoring network is claimed.

MICRO-ONE · One Health surveillance

Metagenomic surveillance for livestock health.

Monitoring soil, water and animals for livestock disease threats entering Europe, the spread of antibiotic resistance and zoonoses.

MICRO-ONE is developing this research programme through a Vinnova-supported planning project led by SLU with Karolinska Institutet.

Research focus

Surveillance of livestock disease and resistance.

The proposed research would analyse genetic material from microbial communities in soil, water and animals to investigate pathogens and antimicrobial resistance.

We aim to connect environmental and animal sampling, including wastewater-based epidemiology, to help detect emerging zoonotic threats and assess disease risks along entry corridors to Europe. One Health links these questions across animal, human and environmental health.

The current grant funds planning and proposal development. The wider surveillance research is proposed for future funding.
